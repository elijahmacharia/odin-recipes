Apple Dump Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 (21-ounce) cans apple pie filling
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 (15.25 ounce) package spice cake mix
  • ¾ cup butter
  • 1 cup chopped pecans

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C.)
  2. Pour apple pie filling into a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  3. Whisk sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and allspice together in a small bowl until well combined; sprinkle over pie filling. Cover filling with dry cake mix; dot with butter, then scatter pecans on top.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until top is browned and filling bubbles on the sides, 45 to 60 minutes.
